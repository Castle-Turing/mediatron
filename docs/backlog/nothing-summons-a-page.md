# Nothing summons a page

Milestone 1 renders a file the resident names. The castle
integration — a status update, brief, or research page opening
*itself* in front of the resident at the right moment — is the
actual point, and it is not designed: how a castle document
declares itself mediatron-renderable, how the castle invokes the
window (and under whose authority, since opening a window spends
attention), and how internal links resolve across repos. Waits on
milestone 1 proving the rendering half.
